## 0.41.0 (beta): first public release

For Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.252+. The prerequisite of High Degree of Freedom Vehicles 0.24.

- Everything up to here: the sea (irregular waves, coasts, wakes), the world's wind and sky, vanilla boat physics,
  aerodynamics, engines and gas dynamics, wheels and drivetrains, the part-based vehicle, rendering and synthesized
  sound (see the CHANGELOG).
- English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese.
- Licence LGPL-3.0-only.
